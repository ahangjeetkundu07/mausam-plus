ABOUT US

Weather Information, Simplified.

MAUSAM+ is a modern weather reporting platform designed to provide clear, reliable and easy-to-understand weather information.

About MAUSAM+

MAUSAM+ brings essential weather information together in one convenient platform. Users can access current weather conditions, forecasts, weather alerts and atmospheric information through a simple and intuitive interface.

Our goal is to make weather information accessible, understandable and useful for everyday decision-making.
